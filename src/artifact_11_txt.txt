[Security Verification Pipeline]
1. Account Identity Gate (seeds, owner, mint constraints)
   └── Validates PDA derivations & eliminates account substitution
2. Signer Invariant Check
   └── Validates caller authority (Creator on Cancel, Solver on Settle)
3. Arithmetic Integrity Invariant
   └── Checked math on all rate conversions, prevents 128-bit overflow
4. Economic Invariant Gate
   └── Uniform price enforcement; out >= min_proportional_out
5. State Monotonicity Gate
   └── Status transitions: Active -> Settled / Cancelled (Strict single terminal transition)
